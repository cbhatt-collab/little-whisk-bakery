Our Story

From one wobbly mixer to a real (small!) bakery

Little Whisk Bakery started on a rainy Saturday with a borrowed recipe card, a wobbly hand mixer and one very determined 12-year-old baker named Maya.

That first batch of chocolate chip cookies was a little flat and a little too brown at the edges, but the neighbors ate every crumb and asked for more. So Maya kept baking. Weekends became test-kitchen days. Notebooks filled up with recipe tweaks: more brown sugar here, a pinch of sea salt there, ten fewer seconds in the oven. Slowly the cookies got chewier, the brownies got fudgier, and a family-favorite tiramisu joined the menu.

Today Little Whisk is a real (small!) business run from a home kitchen, with a grown-up helping with the oven and deliveries. Every order is still made by hand in small batches with real butter, good chocolate and no shortcuts. Nothing sits on a shelf for days. It's baked fresh, boxed with care and finished with a handwritten thank-you note.

Little Whisk is about more than dessert. It's about learning to run a business, being proud of your work and sharing something sweet with your community. Every order helps a young baker save for culinary camp, and maybe one day a real bakery storefront.

Thanks for supporting a small business, and a small baker with big dreams.
